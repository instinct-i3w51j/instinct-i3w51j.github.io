Cyber Law

At the intersection of law and technology - protecting your privacy, reputation and business online

Life has moved online, and the law is there with it. The office works at the intersection of law and technology, combining technological understanding with legal tools to protect your privacy, reputation and business online.

Privacy and personal data protection

Your information is an asset, and harming it harms your privacy. The office handles privacy violations and data leaks, and advises businesses on meeting privacy-law requirements.

Online defamation and reputational harm

A harmful post spreads fast and stays for a long time. The office works to remove harmful content, files defamation claims and helps restore your good name.

Online fraud and cybercrime

Was your account hacked, or were you defrauded online? The office guides you through the legal and practical steps - documentation, approaching the authorities and platforms, and claiming damages.

Terms of use and privacy policies

Every site and app needs proper terms of use and a privacy policy. The office drafts documents tailored to your activity and to legal requirements, so you can operate with peace of mind.
