Accessibility Statement

Rafael Gad Roth Law Office places great importance on providing equal service to all visitors, including people with disabilities, and works to make the firm's website accessible.

Accessibility level

This website aims to conform to Israeli Standard 5568 for web content accessibility, at level AA of the WCAG 2.1 guidelines. This statement is not a certification or approval of conformity.

Accessibility adjustments made on this site

  • Semantic page structure and proper heading hierarchy.
  • Alternative text for images.
  • Full keyboard navigation, including a skip-to-content link and a visible focus indicator.
  • Color contrast that meets the requirements.
  • Plain, clear language and a mobile-friendly layout.
