Tort Law

Representing injured plaintiffs in compensation claims in Jerusalem and across Israel

A road accident, a fall or a medical error can change a life in a moment. The office represents injured clients against those responsible, the insurance companies and the National Insurance Institute, managing every case from the initial assessment to its conclusion - in negotiation or in court.

Medical negligence

A mistake in diagnosis, surgery or treatment can cause irreversible harm. The office builds medical negligence cases on meticulous documentation and expert medical opinions, and accompanies the injured person and their family at every stage.

Road accidents

Even an accident that looks minor can end in long-term damage. The office handles claims against insurance companies and the Road Accident Victims Compensation Fund, reviews your full rights and fights for the compensation you deserve.

Work accidents

Were you injured at work? You deserve a full response - from the National Insurance Institute and, where negligence is involved, from the employer. The office handles the process with all parties so that you do not give up your rights.

Personal injury

A fall in the street, a defect in a public place or a faulty product - personal injuries come in many forms. The office examines who is responsible, what can be claimed and the case's prospects, and gives you an honest picture before you set out.

Insurance and National Insurance claims

Insurance companies deny claims far too easily. The office represents policyholders against insurance companies and the National Insurance Institute, and makes sure your rights do not fall between the cracks.
