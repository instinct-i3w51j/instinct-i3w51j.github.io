Family Law

Sensitive, determined counsel in divorce, custody and support - in Jerusalem and across Israel

Family disputes are among the most sensitive and complex areas of law. The office combines attentive listening with firm advocacy for your rights, explaining your options in plain language so you can make informed decisions.

Divorce and divorce agreements

Divorce changes the whole picture - status, property and children. The office accompanies the process from the first decision to a signed agreement or judgment, making sure you emerge with an arrangement that protects you.

Child support and custody

The child's best interest comes first, along with your financial stability. The office works toward fair custody and support arrangements, in mediation or in court, while preserving healthy parenting relations wherever possible.

Prenuptial and financial agreements

The right financial agreement prevents future disputes. The office drafts agreements before and during marriage, in clear language that sets out both partners' rights and obligations.

Mediation and family dispute resolution

Not every dispute must end in court. The office offers mediation and consensual resolution, saving time, money and harm to the family - and reaching a solution both sides can live with.

Guardianship

When a loved one can no longer care for themselves, a proper legal framework is needed. The office handles guardianship applications, including powers and reporting to the authorities, with sensitivity and professionalism.
