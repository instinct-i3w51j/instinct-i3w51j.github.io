Practice Areas

Tort Law

Injured and entitled to compensation? The office represents plaintiffs in tort claims across Israel. Every case is built on careful evidence and expert opinions, with personal guidance from the first assessment through to the end of the process - in negotiation or in court. Some of our practice areas:

  • Medical Malpractice
  • Car Accidents
  • Work Accidents
  • Personal Injury
  • Insurance & National Insurance Institute Claims
Family Law

Family law calls for sensitivity and resolve. The office accompanies clients in all family law matters, in Jerusalem and across Israel. We explain the options in plain language and build, together with you, a path that protects you and your children. Some of our practice areas:

  • Divorce & Divorce Agreements
  • Child Support & Custody
  • Prenuptial & Financial Agreements
  • Family Mediation & Dispute Resolution
  • Guardianship
Wills & Inheritance

A good will starts with thinking ahead. The office assists in drafting wills that reflect your wishes precisely. We make sure the document meets every legal requirement and preserves your wishes in full. Some of our practice areas:

  • Premium Wills - The Tsirlin Method
  • Will Drafting & Mutual Wills
  • Enduring Power of Attorney
  • Probate & Inheritance Orders
  • Estate Administration & Distribution
  • Will Contests
Cyber Law

Life has moved online, and the law is there with it. Working at the intersection of law and technology, the office handles cyber matters. We combine technological understanding with legal tools to protect your reputation and your business online. Some of our practice areas:

  • Privacy & Personal Data Protection
  • Online Defamation & Reputation
  • Online Fraud & Cybercrime
  • Terms of Use & Privacy Policies for Websites and Apps
Administrative Law

An authority's decision is not necessarily the final word. The office represents individuals and businesses before state and local authorities. We examine the decision, the process and the authority's powers, and build an orderly argument that serves your interests. Some of our practice areas:

  • Petitions & Appeals to Authorities
  • Business Licensing
  • Freedom of Information Requests
  • Representation Before Government & Local Authorities
Real Estate Law

A real estate transaction is usually the biggest deal of a lifetime. The office accompanies property transactions in Jerusalem and across Israel. We examine rights, obstacles and documents in depth, so you can sign with peace of mind. Some of our practice areas:

  • Home & Property Purchase and Sale
  • Lease Agreements
  • Land Registry (Tabu) Registration
  • Purchases from Developers & Buying Groups
  • Urban Renewal: TAMA 38 & Pinui-Binui

And additional practice areas - contact us and we'll look into your matter together.

Our firm combines legal expertise, a deep understanding of people, and technological fluency. We believe good counsel begins with listening and ends with a solution that truly serves the client, guiding every client with confidence at the intersection of law, technology, and people.
