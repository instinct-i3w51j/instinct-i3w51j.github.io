Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to common questions in the office's practice areas. The information is general only and is not a substitute for legal advice.

General

Where in Israel is the office active?

The office is based in Jerusalem and serves clients across Israel - in person, by phone or by video call. We will be glad to examine your matter; contact us to schedule a consultation.

How do I schedule a first consultation?

Reach out by phone, WhatsApp or the form on this site, and we will get back to you promptly to schedule. In the first conversation we will examine the facts and circumstances and recommend the appropriate course of action.

Torts

What should I do after a road accident?

Obtain medical treatment and document the circumstances, and do not sign a waiver or settlement with the insurance company without early legal review. The office guides injured parties in dealing with insurers and acts to file the claim properly and on time.

What is medical negligence and how is it proven?

Medical negligence is the provision of treatment that deviates from the accepted standard of care and causes harm, and proof rests primarily on expert medical opinions. The office guides clients from the review of the complete medical record through representation in the full proceedings.

Wills

How much does it cost to make a will?

The cost depends on the complexity of the will and your personal circumstances. After a short conversation to understand your needs, we will present a clear, itemized quote.

What is the Tsirlin method for drafting a will?

The Tsirlin method is an approach to will drafting that includes planning ahead for future scenarios, aiming for your wishes to be carried out over time and to reduce disputes between heirs. The office drafts wills according to this method, tailored to each client.

What is a lasting power of attorney?

A lasting power of attorney determines in advance who will act on your behalf in financial, medical and other matters, should you become unable to make decisions yourself. The office guides clients in drafting and depositing it, so the appointee is the person you chose rather than one appointed by the court.

Real Estate

What should be checked before signing a real-estate agreement?

Before signing, the Land Registry record - mortgages, liens, foreclosures and cautionary notes - is examined, as well as the planning and building status, tax liabilities and schedules. The office guides clients through a full review of the property and the drafting and tailoring of the agreement before signing.

What is TAMA 38 and how is it relevant to me?

TAMA 38 is a national plan for strengthening older buildings, usually including additions and renovation. The office guides residents in examining the agreement with the developer and tailoring its terms to their rights before signing.

Family

How do divorce proceedings begin?

Divorce proceedings are conducted before the rabbinical court or the family court, and the proceeding filed first may affect the entire process. Early legal guidance assists in choosing the right track and in arranging maintenance, custody and property; the office accompanies clients throughout the process.

What is a financial agreement and who is it for?

A financial agreement sets out in advance how assets will be divided in case of separation, and requires legal approval to be valid. The office guides couples in drafting and approving the agreement, before or during marriage.

Cyber

My account was hacked (email, WhatsApp or social media) - what should I do?

A hacked account requires immediate action: changing passwords, enabling two-factor authentication and assessing the damage - and in cases of extortion or identity misuse, legal steps as well. The office guides victims of cyber attacks from the moment of discovery - with the platforms and relevant parties, and with a complaint to the authorities where needed.

Administrative

I received a report or order for a building violation - what should I do?

A report or order concerning a building violation requires immediate attention, as failing to respond may lead to administrative or criminal proceedings and additional costs. The office guides property owners in examining the order and dealing with the local authority to settle the matter - it is advisable to reach out promptly.
