Real Estate Law

Guiding real-estate transactions in Jerusalem and across Israel

A real-estate transaction is usually the biggest deal of one's life. The office guides transactions in Jerusalem and across Israel, examining rights, obstacles and documents in depth - so that you can sign with confidence.

Purchase and sale of apartments and properties

Buying an apartment is exciting, but also legally complex. The office examines the property and the seller, drafts the contract and accompanies you until the rights are registered in your name.

Lease agreements

A well-drafted lease prevents needless disputes. The office drafts and reviews leases for landlords and tenants, and handles disputes when they arise.

Land-registry (Tabu) registration

Orderly Tabu registration is the final proof of ownership. The office handles rights registration, transfers and registration problems with the Land Registry.

Contractor deals and purchase groups

Buying from a contractor or in a purchase group carries unique risks. The office reviews the guarantees, the timetable and the payments, and makes sure your interest is protected.

Urban renewal: TAMA 38 and demolition-rebuilding

An urban renewal project changes your property for years to come. The office represents tenants in TAMA 38 and demolition-rebuilding projects and ensures they receive their full rights.
