Wills and Inheritance

Wills, lasting powers of attorney and estates - professionally and patiently

A good will starts with forethought. The office guides clients through drafting a will that reflects their wishes precisely, and through everything that follows - from an inheritance order to a will contest.

Premium Wills - The Tsirlin Method

The office offers premium will drafting using the Tsirlin method. For details about the method and how it fits your needs - contact us and we will be glad to explain.

Will drafting and mutual wills

A well-drafted will prevents disputes and ensures your wishes are honored. The office prepares personal wills and mutual wills for couples, and makes sure the document meets every legal requirement.

Lasting power of attorney

A lasting power of attorney lets you decide today who will care for you tomorrow. The office explains the options, drafts the document and guides its proper deposit.

Probate and inheritance orders

Obtaining an inheritance order or probate requires precision and attention to detail. The office handles applications before the Registrar of Inheritance and the courts, shortening the road to a resolution.

Estate administration and distribution

Distributing an estate can become a source of family dispute. The office guides heirs and estate administrators through an orderly distribution of assets, by agreement or in legal proceedings.

Will contests

Concerned that a will does not reflect the testator's true wishes? The office examines the case in depth and protects the heirs' rights, in negotiation or in court.
